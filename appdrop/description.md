**Your COROS recovery, sleep and training at a glance, in home-screen widgets.**

Cairn is widget-first. It shows the numbers your COROS watch has already sent to COROS in small, quiet widgets, and each number carries an honest note about how old it is.

## Widgets first

- Open **Widgets** to pick a widget from a gallery with live previews, or edit the ones already on your home screen.
- Widgets include **Today**, **Recovery**, **Last activity**, **Training load**, **Sleep**, **Fitness**, **Overview**, **Overnight HRV**, **Heart rate**, **Stress** and **Weekly mileage**.
- Each widget has its own editor. The live preview stays pinned at the top while you scroll the options.
- Press and hold any card in Today, Activities or Fitness to add it to your home screen or open COROS.
- Sizes from 2x2 to 4x3, with 11 looks.

## The app

- Tabs: Widgets, Today, Activities and Fitness, named and ordered like the COROS app.
- Every widget has a freshness dot and a sync button. Tapping a widget opens COROS and syncs.
- Manual sync any time, or automatic refresh every 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
- Health Connect as an optional, read-only fallback source.
- Km or miles, light, dark or system mode, and TalkBack and large-font support.
- A compact update notice when a newer version is on AppDrop.

## Honest limits

- Cairn cannot make your watch sync. Data is only as fresh as COROS's last sync from the watch. Open COROS to sync, then Cairn catches up.
- COROS's MCP replies are not a documented stable API, so a COROS change can affect Cairn until it is updated. Cairn shows less rather than wrong values.
- Some values can differ slightly from the COROS app.

## Privacy

- Everything stays on your phone. There is no Cairn server, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.
- You sign in on COROS's own OAuth page. Cairn never sees or stores your password. Tokens are encrypted with the Android Keystore and sent only to COROS.
- Health Connect is read-only.
- Cairn contacts the AppDrop store only to check for updates (at most every 12 hours) and to send feedback if you choose to. No health data is ever sent.

Requires Android 14 or newer. Cairn is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by COROS Wearables Inc.
