**Your COROS recovery, sleep and training at a glance, in home-screen widgets.**

Cairn shows the numbers your COROS watch has already sent to COROS (steps, sleep, recovery, training load and fitness) in small, quiet widgets and a simple app. Each number carries an honest note about how old it is.

## Widgets

- **Today**, **Recovery**, **Last activity**, **Training load** (Intensity Trend), **Sleep**, **Fitness** (race grid) and **Overview** (a grid of 1 to 6 stats you choose).
- 11 looks: Clean (the default), Dusk, Dawn, Forest, Midnight, Sunrise, Minimal, Ocean, Violet, Berry and Transparent.
- A per-widget editor with a live preview, and sizes from 2x2 to 4x3.
- Every widget has a freshness dot (green, amber or red) and a sync button. Tapping a widget opens COROS and syncs.

## The app

- Today, Activities, Fitness, Settings and Diagnostics, named and ordered like the COROS app.
- Manual sync any time, or automatic refresh every 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
- Health Connect as an optional, read-only fallback source.
- Km or miles, light, dark or system mode, TalkBack labels and large-font support.

## Honest limits

- Cairn cannot make your watch sync. Data is only as fresh as COROS's last sync from the watch. Open COROS to sync, then Cairn catches up.
- COROS's MCP replies are not a documented stable API. Cairn shows less rather than wrong values, but a COROS change can break things until Cairn is updated.
- Some values can differ slightly from the COROS app, and there is no true time-in-zone data.
- Background refreshes can be delayed by battery saver. The footer always shows the real age.

## Privacy

- Everything stays on your phone. There is no Cairn server, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.
- You sign in on COROS's own OAuth page. Cairn never sees or stores your password. Tokens are encrypted with the Android Keystore, excluded from backups and sent only to COROS.
- Health Connect is read-only.
- Cairn contacts the AppDrop store only to check for updates and to send feedback if you choose to. No health data is ever sent.

Requires Android 14 or newer. Cairn is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by COROS Wearables Inc.
